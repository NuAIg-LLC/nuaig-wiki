## If you only have five minutes

<ol class="steps not-content">
  <li>
    <h4>A community needs to know whether a prospective resident's money will last.</h4>
    <p>They pay privately. If the money runs out, the contract often says the resident stays anyway, and the shortfall is absorbed by everyone else.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>Staff collect the financial picture, from the family and from documents.</h4>
    <p>A family completes a guided application from home; staff verify every figure before it counts.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>A versioned, deterministic engine projects the whole residency, month by month.</h4>
    <p>Same inputs, same answer, forever. No clock reads, no floating-point money, no model guessing a number.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>The result is an explainable outcome, not a verdict.</h4>
    <p>Every factor names the rule that produced it, and the engine and policy versions are stamped on the result.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>A named human records the decision, bound to that exact result.</h4>
    <p>With a written rationale, inside an append-only audit trail that can be independently verified.</p>
  </li>
</ol>

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>What the software does</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Collects and verifies the financial picture</li>
      <li>Projects the residency and the funding gap</li>
      <li>Explains every factor and names its rule</li>
      <li>Routes the case through an approval chain</li>
      <li>Records who decided, and on what basis</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>What the software never does</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Decline, waitlist or approve anybody</li>
      <li>Let a language model set or change a number</li>
      <li>Show raw financial detail to a role without the scope</li>
      <li>Reach across a client or community boundary</li>
      <li>Quietly re-price or re-rate an existing record</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
