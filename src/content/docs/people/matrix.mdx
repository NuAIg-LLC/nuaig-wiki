Read across to see what one role can do, and down to see who can do one thing. The
matrix is derived directly from the role-to-scope assignments. Where a cell says **Partly**,
the exact condition is in the notes below the table.

## What "Partly" means

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Open uploaded documents (counselor)</dt><dd>The counselor can open a document and see that it exists, what kind it is and when it arrived. The content of a financial document, its extracted text and its figures require the financial scope. Medical documents require the health scope.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Derived assessment result (counselor, auditor)</dt><dd>The tier, the runway in months and the fired rules are visible. Dollar figures are replaced with a withheld marker, and factors built from health detail are removed for anyone without the health scope.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Unit quotes (executive director)</dt><dd>Can read quotes and prior-sale history, since they hold all three read scopes. Creating a quote also needs permission to run assessments, which the executive director does not hold.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Review prerequisites</dt><dd>Which roles may complete each prerequisite is configured per organisation. The matrix shows the two roles that are eligible; a given client may allow only one.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Waivers and escalations</dt><dd>Only a finance reviewer or executive director who is also on the organisation's named approver list. Holding the role is not enough, and nobody may answer their own request.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Cross-community reports</dt><dd>Requires an explicit grant per community from a client administrator, plus a live membership in that community with the financial, health and assessment read scopes. The grant alone creates no access.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Audit trail (client administrator)</dt><dd>Can read the organisation's activity feed. Reading the trail of one particular resident additionally requires resident read access, which an administrator does not have, so that request is refused.</dd></div>
</div>

## Emergency access

The break-glass role is not shown, because it grants nothing by itself. While a time-boxed
grant is active, a person gains the **read** half of every scope and none of the write half.
See <a href="/people/emergency-access/">Emergency access</a>.
