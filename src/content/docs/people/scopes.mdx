A **scope** is the smallest unit of permission in the product. It is a short phrase of the
form `resource:action`, such as `financials:read` or `decisions:write`. A role is nothing
more than a named bundle of scopes, and every check in the product asks "does this person
hold this scope?" rather than "is this person a finance reviewer?".

This is why roles can be reshuffled without touching the rules, and why the rules can be
read in one place.

## The seventeen scopes

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt><code>prospects:read</code></dt><dd>See a resident record at summary level: identity and pipeline state. Held by counselors, sales reviewers, finance reviewers, executive directors and auditors.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>prospects:write</code></dt><dd>Create and edit resident records. Held by counselors and sales reviewers.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>financials:read</code></dt><dd>See raw financial detail: balances, income, extracted amounts. Deliberately separate from summary reads. Held by sales reviewers, finance reviewers and executive directors.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>phi:read</code></dt><dd>See care level, diagnosis and acuity. Held by counselors, sales reviewers, finance reviewers and executive directors.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>assessments:read</code></dt><dd>See the derived result of an assessment, filtered by what else the person may see. Held by every role that works with residents.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>assessments:run</code></dt><dd>Execute a calculation and complete a qualification. Held by sales reviewers and finance reviewers.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>assessments:override</code></dt><dd>Override an assessment result, with a reason. Held by finance reviewers only.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>documents:read</code></dt><dd>Open uploaded evidence. What is visible inside depends on the document's class; see <a href="/people/minimum-necessary/">Minimum necessary access</a>.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>documents:write</code></dt><dd>Upload, review and verify documents. Held by counselors, sales reviewers and finance reviewers.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>decisions:read</code></dt><dd>Read recorded decisions. Held by sales reviewers, finance reviewers, executive directors and auditors.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>decisions:write</code></dt><dd>Record the human-of-record decision. Held by the executive director and nobody else.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>audit:read</code></dt><dd>Read the audit trail. Held by executive directors, auditors and client administrators. Reading a single resident's trail additionally needs <code>prospects:read</code>.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>applications:manage</code></dt><dd>Create family applications, send and revoke invitations, and review submissions.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>applications:promote</code></dt><dd>Accept a submitted application into the pipeline as a resident record.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>users:manage</code></dt><dd>Manage staff accounts. Held by client administrators.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>tenant:admin</code></dt><dd>Configure the organisation: communities, policy, pricing, portal, access. Held by client administrators. Grants no resident-data access.</dd></div>
  <div><dt><code>platform:admin</code></dt><dd>Provision and manage client organisations. Held by platform administrators only, who carry no other scope.</dd></div>
</div>

## Why financial and health reads are separate

Seeing that a person exists is not the same as seeing their money, and neither is the same
as seeing their diagnosis. The product treats these as three different disclosures:

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>Summary</h4>
    <p><code>prospects:read</code> and <code>assessments:read</code>. Who the person is and where the case stands, plus the derived result with dollar figures withheld.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>Financial</h4>
    <p><code>financials:read</code>. Balances, income and the figures inside financial documents. Needed for anything that reasons about money.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>Health</h4>
    <p><code>phi:read</code>. Care level, diagnosis and acuity. Needed for the life-plan workspace as well, since care level drives cost.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Anything that combines money and care, such as a life-plan projection, a qualification, a
report row or a quote, requires **all three** read scopes together. A role missing any one
of them is refused outright rather than shown a partial view.

## Two rules about administration

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Administration is not access</dt><dd><code>tenant:admin</code> configures the product and carries no resident scope. A client administrator who also needs to read residents must hold a separate, explicit membership with a resident-facing role.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Emergency access is read-only</dt><dd>An active emergency grant adds the read half of the scope list and nothing else. It cannot add a write scope, and it never crosses an organisation boundary.</dd></div>
</div>
