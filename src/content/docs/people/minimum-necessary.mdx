**Minimum necessary** is the principle that a person receives only the information their
job requires. In this product it is not a styling choice. It is enforced at the moment a
response is assembled, so that data a role may not see is never transmitted to it.

## Why hiding it on screen is not enough

If a financial figure is sent to the browser and then hidden with styling, it has been
disclosed. Anyone with the browser's developer tools can read it. So the product does the
opposite: the figure is removed from the response before it leaves the records service.

## What each kind of information requires

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Identity</dt><dd>Name and basic details. Visible to anyone who can see the resident at all.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Care level and health detail</dt><dd>Needs the health scope. Factors in a result that are built from health detail are removed entirely for anyone without it, not softened.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Financial figures</dt><dd>Needs the financial scope. Without it, dollar amounts inside free text, such as a plain-language summary, are replaced with a withheld marker.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Derived results</dt><dd>The tier, the months of runway and the rules that fired. Visible to anyone who may read assessments, because they describe the outcome without the underlying money.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Identity numbers</dt><dd>Never returned in full to any role. Only the last four digits are ever shown, and the full number is held apart from the resident record in a separate store.</dd></div>
</div>

## Documents are gated by what is inside them

A document is classified by its **kind**, and the kind decides who may read its content.

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Financial kinds</dt><dd>Bank and brokerage statements, tax returns, pay stubs, property valuations, mortgage statements, trust documents, benefit letters, insurance policies and benefit notices. Content, extracted text and extracted figures all require the financial scope.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Medical kinds</dt><dd>Physician statements. Require the health scope.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Legal kinds</dt><dd>Power of attorney and guardianship papers. Readable with ordinary document access.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Other or unrecognised</dt><dd>Could contain anything, so reading one requires <strong>both</strong> the financial and the health scope. A document with no recognised kind fails closed the same way.</dd></div>
</div>

The kind a person declares and the kind the assistant infers both count. Relabelling a
document can therefore never lower the protection it already had.

A counselor can see that a document exists, what it is called, and when it arrived. They
cannot open its contents or see the figures extracted from it. They run the conversation
with the family while finance owns the numbers.

## How the screen behaves

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>It never offers a refused action</h4>
    <p>A button that the records service would reject is not shown. The screen is built from the same rules, so the two cannot disagree.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>It explains instead of refusing</h4>
    <p>A blocked step names its owner and the reason, and says what to ask for. "Forbidden" on its own helps nobody.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>It declares what was withheld</h4>
    <p>A response states that a mask was applied, so a screen and an integration can both tell "no data" from "not allowed to see it".</p>
  </div>
</div>
