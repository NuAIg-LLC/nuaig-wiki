A senior-living organisation runs communities where residents pay for their own care,
often for the rest of their lives. Before anyone moves in, somebody has to answer a
question with a great deal of money attached to it:

> Will this household's money last for as long as they live here? If it will not, when
> does it run out, by how much, and who decided that was acceptable?

This platform answers that question. It collects the household's financial picture,
verifies it against documents, projects the whole residency month by month, classifies
the result against thresholds the client's finance leadership has approved, routes the
case through a named approval chain, and records who signed, on what evidence, and when.

  <span><b>Sector</b>Senior living, admissions and finance</span>
  <span><b>Users</b>Community staff, plus the applicant's family</span>
  <span><b>Decides</b>Nothing. A named person does.</span>

The **staff workspace** is where the work happens: intake, document review, financial
verification, projection, approval and reporting. The **family portal** exists because
families need to send sensitive financial and medical information from home without
being given a staff account. That makes it the highest-risk surface in the product, so
it is isolated twice over rather than trusted once.

## What it produces

The product's output is not a score. It is a **qualification**: a frozen record that
contains everything needed to reproduce the answer years later.

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>The inputs</dt><dd>The household exactly as it was entered and verified: residents, income streams, assets, expenses, insurance benefits.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>The policy</dt><dd>The version of the client's assumptions in force at the time: growth rates, care durations, contract catalogue, thresholds.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>The pricing</dt><dd>The fee schedule and, where one was used, the exact unit quote the family was shown.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>The calculation</dt><dd>The identified version of the calculation model that produced the figures.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>The result</dt><dd>The month-by-month projection, the ending balance, any funding shortfall, the contingent refund, and the classified outcome.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>The people</dt><dd>Who verified the financial inputs, who recommended, who approved, and who signed.</dd></div>
</div>

A qualification is immutable. Re-running produces a new one beside it. Nothing is edited
away, because this record is evidence.

## The line the product will not cross

A qualification is **not an admission decision**. The platform informs a human approval
chain and records the outcome of that chain, but it never declines, waitlists or approves
anybody.

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel panel--ok">
    <h4>The software is responsible for</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Arithmetic that reproduces exactly, every time</li>
      <li>Explaining which rule produced every figure</li>
      <li>Showing each person only what their role allows</li>
      <li>Refusing an action the record would not support</li>
      <li>Recording who did what, in a trail nobody can edit</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--stop">
    <h4>A person is responsible for</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Confirming that the figures match the evidence</li>
      <li>Judging whether a marginal case is acceptable</li>
      <li>Recommending, approving or declining</li>
      <li>Signing the final decision, with a written reason</li>
      <li>Approving the assumptions the model runs on</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

## Why it is built the way it is

One resident record concentrates three of the most heavily regulated classes of data at
the same time: personal identity, health information, and non-public financial
information. The product therefore treats that record as its principal liability rather
than its principal asset. Three consequences run through every page of this wiki.

<ol class="steps not-content">
  <li>
    <h4>Collect the minimum, and show less than that.</h4>
    <p>The same screen returns different fields to different roles, and the response states that a mask was applied. An admissions counselor can run the conversation without ever seeing a balance.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>Make the arithmetic reproducible, not merely correct.</h4>
    <p>Money is held as whole cents, never as decimal fractions. No calculation reads today's date. A projection replayed in ten years returns the same figures to the cent.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>Make every access visible.</h4>
    <p>Every read and write of regulated data is appended to a tamper-evident trail that can be verified independently, so a quietly edited or deleted event is detectable.</p>
  </li>
</ol>
