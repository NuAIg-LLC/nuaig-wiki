## The areas

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Overview</dt><dd>The organisation, its onboarding status, how many facilities and staff accounts it has, any facility with nobody holding active access, and a "needs attention" panel. For example, if no assessment policy has been published, it says every facility is scoring against the built-in defaults and links to the policy.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Profile</dt><dd>The organisation's name and address slug.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Facilities</dt><dd>Buildings, their status and who works in them. Adding one.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Staff</dt><dd>Accounts, roles and which facilities each person can access. Inviting a colleague.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Qualification report access</dt><dd>Explicit community grants for leadership reports.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Emergency access</dt><dd>Break-glass grants, who holds one and why.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Activity log</dt><dd>Who changed what, and when. See <a href="/screens/activity/">Activity log</a>.</dd></div>
</div>

### Configuration

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Assessment policy</dt><dd>Risk thresholds, horizons, escalation and Medicaid rules centrally, and monthly pricing per facility.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Life-plan configuration</dt><dd>Contracts, duration profiles, thresholds and rates centrally, and prices per community.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Qualification review policy</dt><dd>Independent requirements, committee and exception approvers.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Unit inventory and prices</dt><dd>Selected-community units and dated price schedules.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Price reduction approvers</dt><dd>Named finance and director approval authority.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Family portal</dt><dd>Branding, welcome text, contact person and the sections and documents requested.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Communications</dt><dd>Whether outbound messages are sent, and the gateways for email and text.</dd></div>
</div>

## How the editors behave

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>The address names the target</h4>
    <p>An editor for a policy or a portal takes its target from its address, never from the building selected in the top bar. Two administrators following one link edit the same thing.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>Structured controls</h4>
    <p>Editors are forms, not a box of raw settings. Blank financial fields stay blank, and nothing is supplied as an approved default.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>A draft is pinned</h4>
    <p>The editor remembers the revision it loaded. A failed publish never quietly upgrades it to a newer one. Publishing a stale draft is a conflict the person reviews.</p>
  </div>
</div>
