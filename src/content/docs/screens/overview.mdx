## What every screen has in common

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>It never offers a refused action</dt><dd>A button the records service would reject is not shown. Where a step belongs to someone else, the screen names the owner and the reason.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>It names what it edits</dt><dd>Administrative editors take their target from the address, never from whichever community happens to be selected in the top bar, so two people following one link edit the same thing.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>A conflict is shown, not hidden</dt><dd>Every editor remembers the version it loaded. If someone else changed the record, the screen says so and asks the person to review. It never overwrites and never retries on its own.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Drafts live in the page</dt><dd>Sensitive drafts are not written to the browser's storage, a cookie or the address. They disappear when the person leaves.</dd></div>
</div>
