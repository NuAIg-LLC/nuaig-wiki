## The action form

The form adapts to the person. It lists the review types **they** may record, and for each
shows the evidence needed, any exception reference, and the option to correct an earlier
note. Every action carries a reason. The screen pins the policy revision it loaded, so
a policy change is shown as a conflict, never silently absorbed.

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Required reviews</dt><dd>Each with a code, a label and the roles allowed to complete it. Added or removed with structured controls rather than a raw text box.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Review lifetime</dt><dd>How many hours a review stays valid.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Committee slots</dt><dd>Optional, by role or by named person.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Exception approvers</dt><dd>Chosen from active assigned staff. An empty list grants no authority.</dd></div>
</div>

Publishing goes through the central control only. Selecting a target never changes which
residents anyone can see. An unpublished draft keeps its original revision and its
idempotency key until it is explicitly discarded or successfully published.

See <a href="/flows/decision/">Review, sign-off and decision</a> for the rules the screen enforces.
