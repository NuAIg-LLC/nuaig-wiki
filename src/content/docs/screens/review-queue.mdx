## What it is for

When a document's text could not be read with enough confidence, a person has to look at
it. The review queue gathers every such document in the community so the reviewer does not
have to open residents one by one to find them.

## How it behaves

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Ordering</dt><dd>By how long the document has waited, or by how confident the extraction was, so the least certain or the oldest can be handled first.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Filtering</dt><dd>A search, and a filter by document type, such as bank statements only.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Opening a document</dt><dd>Takes the reviewer to the document screen, where they confirm or correct what was read. See <a href="/screens/documents/">Documents and extraction</a>.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Content is still gated</dt><dd>A reviewer sees the content of a document only if they hold the scope its kind requires.</dd></div>
</div>

## Why it is separate from the pipeline

The pipeline answers "which residents need attention". The queue answers "which pieces of
evidence need a person". They are different questions with different owners, and mixing them
would bury one under the other.
