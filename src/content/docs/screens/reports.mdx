## Running a report

<Shot
  src="/img/shots/reports-run.webp"
  route="/reports/life-plan"
  role="Finance reviewer"
  caption="<b>Qualification reports.</b> Communities to include, from and through dates, and results per page. After running, a summary and the completed qualifications appear."
/>

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Communities to include</dt><dd>Only the communities this person is currently allowed to report on appear as choices. Up to twenty can be selected.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Dates</dt><dd>From and through, defaulting to the last thirty days. The note beneath explains that dates include the full selected days in UTC and that a period may not exceed 366 days.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Results per page</dt><dd>A page size, with a maximum of fifty.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Before the first run</dt><dd>A short instruction says to select the communities and run the report. Nothing is run automatically.</dd></div>
</div>

## What the results show

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>This page of results</dt><dd>Counts of qualifications, projected shortfalls, qualifications with observations, and those receiving funding support. They are labelled as describing the rows shown, not the whole date range.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Completed qualifications</dt><dd>For each: the resident and community, when it was completed, the planning outcome, the ending available funds with the contingent refund beneath, and the latest observed outcome with its month.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Paging</dt><dd>The exact filters survive moving between pages.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Errors</dt><dd>A failed read is shown as an error, never as an empty result.</dd></div>
</div>

## Granting access

<Shot
  src="/img/shots/report-access.webp"
  route="/admin/report-access"
  role="Client administrator"
  caption="<b>Qualification report access.</b> Pick an eligible staff member and a community and grant access. The page states that a grant does not create a membership or widen a role."
/>

Eligible people are active users with an active membership in an active community holding
the financial, health and assessment read scopes. Platform administrators and emergency
identities cannot be granted it. Grants carry a version, conflicts prompt a reload and
review, and revocation stays available even after membership is removed.

See <a href="/flows/reporting/">Leadership reporting</a>.
