## What it is

A family often needs something to take home and think about. This is that, and nothing
more. It is a **financial planning summary**, prepared by staff from a completed
qualification, that a family can read without logging in to anything.

## Why it is a whitelist

A summary meant for outsiders must not leak what is meant for insiders. The page is
assembled from an explicit list of what may appear, rather than from the full record with
parts hidden. A field that is not on the list is never fetched.

    </ul>

## How the refund is worded

The summary states the refund plainly and shows it apart from the ending balance. Its
wording says that **both residents must leave and the unit must be resold**, and that the
refund cannot pay for ongoing care. A family should never be left thinking it is cash they
can spend.

## How it is shared

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Staff prepare it</dt><dd>The page is an authenticated staff screen. Staff use the browser's Print or Save as PDF.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Turn off browser headers</dt><dd>The page reminds staff to do this, so that the internal address of the staff page is not printed on the document.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Shared through an approved channel</dt><dd>The product does not email it automatically and publishes no public link. The recipient receives a document, not an account.</dd></div>
</div>

## A fair caveat

The care phases and the monthly arithmetic are planning conventions. They are not a clinical
prediction. The summary is meant to help a family understand how the numbers work out, not
to promise how any one life will unfold.
