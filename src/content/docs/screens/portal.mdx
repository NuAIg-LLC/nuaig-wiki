## Why a separate application

Families need to send sensitive financial and medical information from home, without being
given a staff account. That makes this the highest-risk surface in the product. So it is not
trusted once. It is isolated twice.

## Helpers

A family member can invite a helper with an explicit, narrow grant: which sections, which
document types, and whether they may submit. See <a href="/flows/intake/">Intake and the family application</a>.

## What staff control about it

A client administrator sets the community's name, accent colour, welcome text and contact
person, and which sections and document types are requested. Publishing uses the revision
the editor loaded, so two administrators cannot overwrite one another by accident.
