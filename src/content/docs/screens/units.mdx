## Unit inventory

<Shot
  src="/img/shots/units.webp"
  route="/admin/units"
  role="Client administrator"
  caption="<b>Unit inventory.</b> The units in the selected community with their type and availability, and when the source was last checked. Below, a form adds a unit with where the information came from."
/>

A unit has a code that is unique within its community, a label, a type and an availability.
It also records its **source**: the kind of source, a document or import reference, and the
date it was checked. Changing a unit needs the revision the administrator saw.

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Publish prices</dt><dd>A complete schedule effective from a date, with an entrance fee and a monthly independent-living fee for each contract. Contracts and refund percentages come from the central catalogue and the community's assignment.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Prior sales</dt><dd>The actual historical contract, refund rate, entrance price and outstanding refund obligation, each with a source. To correct one, the administrator picks it from a list, and the correction is appended as a new revision.</dd></div>
</div>

## Price reduction approvers

<Shot
  src="/img/shots/unit-approvers.webp"

  role="Client administrator"
  caption="<b>Designated approvers.</b> The page says explicit assignments are required, then lists eligible staff with their role and community. Publishing a new list means existing reduction quotes must be re-requested."

An administrator selects specific people with a live finance reviewer or executive director
membership. Sales staff cannot be designated through their sales role, and nobody is
assigned automatically.
