## For someone who may confirm the numbers

<Shot
  src="/img/shots/financials-finance.webp"
  route="/prospects/{id}  (Financials step)"
  role="Finance reviewer"
  caption="<b>Confirm the numbers.</b> A panel of figures drawn from verified documents, each with a Use this control and the document it came from, then the fields a reviewer confirms or corrects."
/>

The step opens with the figures the verified documents support, such as a monthly benefit,
monthly income and liquid assets, each linked to its source document. A reviewer can use a
figure with one action, or use all of them, and then adjusts or fills the rest.

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Monthly income and liquid assets</dt><dd>The two figures the runway calculation rests on.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Long-term care benefit</dt><dd>The monthly benefit and how many months remain, or that it continues without limit.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Home equity</dt><dd>Whether it counts, and how much. Home value is distinct from equity.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Family contribution</dt><dd>A recurring contribution from a responsible party.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Recent transfers</dt><dd>Amounts moved recently and when the largest one happened, which feeds the look-back check.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Planned length of stay</dt><dd>The horizon the funding gap is measured over.</dd></div>
</div>

Saving **verifies** the profile. That is the step that allows an assessment to run: the
calculation refuses to run on a profile no person has confirmed.

## For someone who may not

<Shot
  src="/img/shots/financials-counselor.webp"
  route="/prospects/{id}  (Financials step)"
  role="Admissions counselor"
  caption="<b>The same address, a different answer.</b> The step is marked as handled by finance, the counselor is told they do not have access to these figures, and the panel says who does this next."
/>

The counselor is not shown an error. The rail marks the step as belonging to finance, the
panel says that a finance reviewer handles it, names who does this next, and offers a way
to move on to the next step they can act on.

## Why it is built this way

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>The numbers are an input, not a fact</h4>
    <p>Everything on this step is something a person has to stand behind. The assistant and the family propose, and this step is where a person disposes.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>Sources stay attached</h4>
    <p>A figure keeps a link to the document it came from, so a later reviewer can see why it was believed.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <h4>Authority is real, not cosmetic</h4>
    <p>The counselor's view is not the finance view with fields hidden. The records service does not send the figures to a role that may not have them.</p>
  </div>
</div>
