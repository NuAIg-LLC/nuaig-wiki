<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Outcome</dt><dd>Approve, decline, waitlist, or request more information.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Reasoning</dt><dd>Required. It becomes part of the permanent record, so the screen asks for the call, the numbers and the situation, in the person's own words.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>The assessment it rests on</dt><dd>The decision is bound to the latest assessment. If the figures, the policy or the calculation have changed since, the decision is refused inside the same transaction as the insert, and a fresh assessment is required.</dd></div>
</div>

## Once recorded

<Shot
  src="/img/shots/decision-recorded.webp"

The recorded decision is shown as a permanent entry. It cannot be edited or deleted, the
resident moves to the decided stage, and that stage cannot be moved back by hand.

## What it refuses

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel panel--stop">
    <h4>Anyone but the director</h4>
    <p>For everyone else the step explains that the director records the outcome and tells them to ask. The capability is held by one role and checked by the records service.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--stop">
    <h4>A decision without a reason</h4>
    <p>The reasoning is required. An empty one is rejected.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--stop">
    <h4>A decision on stale facts</h4>
    <p>A decision citing an assessment that is no longer the current one, or whose inputs have moved, is refused.</p>
  </div>
</div>

For lifetime-contract qualifications, the decision is made through an approval chain and a
stricter signing step. See <a href="/screens/workflow/">Qualification workflow</a> and
<a href="/flows/decision/">Review, sign-off and decision</a>.
