## The Documents step

<Shot
  src="/img/shots/resident-documents.webp"
  route="/prospects/{id}  (Documents step)"
  role="Finance reviewer"
  caption="<b>Documents on a resident.</b> A drop zone for new files, then each document with its type, its age, and its status. Here all three are verified and the step offers to continue to financials."

Files are dropped in, or chosen. The product reads them and sorts them by type. Each row
shows what the file is, how long ago it arrived and where it stands: pending, needs review,
verified or rejected.

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>How to read it</dt><dd>A short guide at the top explains that extraction is a proposal and that the reviewer is the authority.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Extracted fields</dt><dd>Each value the assistant found, such as a balance, with the confidence it assigned. Low confidence is called out rather than hidden.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Anomalies</dt><dd>Things worth a human's attention. For example, that the extraction was made with low confidence and must be human-verified before use.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Reviewer notes</dt><dd>A free-text box for a correction or a reason for rejecting.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Verify or reject</dt><dd>The two outcomes. Verified documents can inform a calculation. Rejected ones cannot.</dd></div>
</div>

## Who sees what

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel panel--ok">
    <h4>Can see the content</h4>
    <p>Someone with the scope the document's kind requires: the financial scope for a bank statement, the health scope for a physician statement. A document of unknown kind needs both.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--stop">
    <h4>Sees only that it exists</h4>
    <p>A counselor sees the file name, type and arrival time, but not the text or the figures read from it. They are told who to ask.</p>
  </div>
</div>

The boundary extends to the extraction itself: a counselor sees the document, never the
dollar values inside it. See <a href="/people/minimum-necessary/">Minimum necessary access</a>
and <a href="/flows/documents/">Documents and verification</a>.
