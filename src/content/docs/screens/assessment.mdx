## Before it runs

If no assessment exists, the step presents a single action to run one. It is available only
to roles that may run assessments, and only once the financial profile has been verified.
While it runs, the button shows progress and cannot be pressed twice.

## Reading a result

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>The headline</dt><dd>A gauge showing the runway in months, the risk tier as a labelled badge, and a one-line explanation. The tier is the colour and the word, never the colour alone.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Monthly shortfall and Medicaid crossover</dt><dd>What is going out each month beyond income, and how soon countable assets reach the state limit.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Plain-language summary</dt><dd>A short paragraph drafted from the figures, written so it can be read aloud to a family. It states the approach and a caveat that the result is a screening aid and not a decision.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Factors</dt><dd>Each is a named rule with a one-sentence reason: for example, that income does not cover the monthly cost, or that a recent transfer falls inside the look-back window. Severities distinguish what is adverse from what merely deserves a look.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Care factors</dt><dd>The projected care trajectory affects the projection, not the tier.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>The stamp</dt><dd>At the foot, the exact calculation version and policy version. A result stays reproducible even after the policy changes.</dd></div>
</div>

## What a different role sees

The result is served to every role that may read assessments, but filtered to what they
may see. Without the financial scope, dollar amounts inside the summary and the factors
are replaced with a withheld marker, and a banner says a mask was applied. Without the
health scope, factors built from care detail are removed entirely.

## What it will not do

<div class="split not-content">
  <div class="panel panel--stop">
    <h4>Decide anything</h4>
    <p>The tier describes the finances. It is not an admission decision, and the screen says a person owns that decision.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--stop">
    <h4>Run on unverified figures</h4>
    <p>Until a person has confirmed the profile, there is nothing to run on.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel panel--stop">
    <h4>Let the assistant change a number</h4>
    <p>The summary is text only. It sits beside the result and never inside it.</p>
  </div>
</div>
