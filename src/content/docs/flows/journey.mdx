## The seven steps

<ol class="steps not-content">
  <li>
    <h4>Counselor starts the case</h4>
    <p>Either by creating the resident record directly, or by inviting the family to apply. An invitation is a secure link sent by email or text to a named contact. See <a href="/flows/intake/">Intake and the family application</a>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>The family completes the guided application</h4>
    <p>They open the link, prove who they are with a one-time code, answer the requested sections, and upload documents. Everything lands in a holding area the family role can reach, never in the resident's record.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>Staff review the submission</h4>
    <p>Each document is read, its type confirmed and its figures verified by a person. Validating the numbers needs the financial scope, so a counselor can run the conversation while finance confirms the money. See <a href="/flows/documents/">Documents and verification</a>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>Staff promote the submission</h4>
    <p>Promotion creates the resident record, copies the verified documents across and carries the validated profile with it. Nothing is promoted unvalidated, and after this the family portal has no further reach into the record.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>A reviewer runs the qualification</h4>
    <p>The household is entered, a projection is calculated against the client's policy and pricing, scenarios are compared, and the reviewer attests that the inputs were verified. See <a href="/flows/qualification/">Life-plan qualification</a>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>The approval chain responds</h4>
    <p>Prerequisites are completed or waived, a sales recommendation is recorded, any committee approvals are given, and any escalation is resolved. See <a href="/flows/decision/">Review, sign-off and decision</a>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>The executive director records the decision</h4>
    <p>Approve, decline, waitlist or request more information, with a written reason, bound to the exact qualification it rests on. A stale reference is refused inside the same transaction.</p>
  </li>
</ol>
