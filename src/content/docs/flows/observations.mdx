A forecast is only worth trusting if someone checks it. Observations are how the product
learns whether the plan held, and the discipline that makes them honest is simple:
**the forecast is never moved to meet the facts.**

An observation asks for facts and nothing else:

<div class="defs not-content">
  <div><dt>Observation date</dt><dd>When the fact was true. A date in the future is rejected.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Months since the projection start</dt><dd>An explicit number. It is never inferred from the date, because completion time is not necessarily the move-in date. Month zero means immediately after the entrance fee.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Verified available funds</dt><dd>The net amount actually available to support care. Do not include an entrance-fee refund that is still contingent on residents leaving.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Funding support</dt><dd>None, partial or full. It is an observation, never inferred from the balance.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Residency status</dt><dd>Resident or exited.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Verification</dt><dd>A fresh attestation every time, and optional staff notes.</dd></div>
</div>

Blank stays blank. A field left empty is never turned into zero or into "none". Dollar
amounts are converted using exact integer arithmetic and checked against the safe range.

## What the comparison shows

The product looks up the original forecast's balance at the stated month and reports the
difference. Two cases are reported honestly instead of being forced into a number:

## Corrections

An observation can be corrected, but never edited. A correction is a **new entry that
references the earlier one**. It keeps the original date and month, asks for an explanation
and a fresh verification, and leaves the earlier entry visible. The store cannot update or
delete observations at all.

## Why this matters

Observations are the only way the product can answer the question leadership will
eventually ask: of the households we qualified, how many did what we expected? Because the
forecast stays frozen and the observations only ever accumulate, the answer is built on
data nobody can have tidied up.

reaches a report.
